# dom-locators

Vitest browser mode's `page` and its locators, with their waiting and their actions, answered inside the document under test: no selector engine of the provider, no round trip to it, no Testing Library.

A test of `vitest/browser` changes one import:

```diff
- import { page } from "vitest/browser";
+ import { page } from "dom-locators";

  await page.getByLabelText("Title").fill("Groceries");
  await page.getByRole("button", { name: "Create note" }).click();
  await expect.element(page.getByText("Note created.")).toBeInTheDocument();
```

A private package of this repository, for its playgrounds and its benchmark: not on npm.

## The contract

For every locator it accepts, `dom-locators` returns the elements that Vitest's `page` returns, in the same order. A locator, an option or a method it does not model throws `UnsupportedError` with the reason. It never returns another element.

The roles, the ARIA states, the accessible names and the text matching are Playwright's, ported from the engine Vitest 5's locators run (ivya 1.8.2, Playwright at 4554372e, see [NOTICE](NOTICE)). The package's own suite holds every locator of a table, on an ARIA page and on the routes of the notes app, to `vitest/browser`'s `page`: the same elements, the same `selector`, the same `asLocator()`.

Open shadow roots are looked into, as Vitest's locators do and in their order: a tree's elements, then the trees of its shadow roots, each host's in the order of the document. A closed shadow root is nobody's: neither finds what is in it. Next's `<next-route-announcer>`, on every page of the App Router, is one.

Vitest's defaults apply: `exact` is Vitest's `browser.locators.exact` (`true` unless your config says otherwise) and `getByTestId()` reads `browser.locators.testIdAttribute`.

## What there is

- `page`: `getByRole`, `getByText`, `getByLabelText`, `getByPlaceholder`, `getByAltText`, `getByTitle`, `getByTestId`, `elementLocator(element)`, `extend(methods)`.
- A locator: the same `getBy*`, `filter({ hasText, hasNotText, has, hasNot })`, `nth`, `first`, `last`, `and`, `or`, `all`, `element`, `elements`, `query`, `length`, `findElement({ timeout, strict })`, `selector`, `toString`, `asLocator`, `serialize`, `toJSON`.
- Actions: `click`, `dblClick`, `tripleClick`, `hover`, `unhover`, `fill`, `clear`, `selectOptions`, `upload` of `File`s, `wheel`, `dropTo` of what the page drags with the mouse's events, and two that Vitest's `Locator` does not have, Playwright's `press(key)` and `pressSequentially(text)`. `press` takes `"Enter"`, `"Escape"`, `"Tab"`, `"Backspace"`, `"Delete"`, `"Space"` or `" "`, the arrows, `"Home"`, `"End"`, a modifier or one printable character, after the modifiers held for it: `"Shift+Tab"`, `"ControlOrMeta+a"`.
- The options of an action are Playwright's that the page can model: `timeout` for every one; `force` and `noWaitAfter` (no effect, as in Playwright) for all but `wheel`; `modifiers`, `position` and `trial` for the mouse's, `sourcePosition`, `targetPosition` and `trial` for `dropTo`; `button` for the clicks; `clickCount` for `click`. Any other option throws, and so does an invalid value. With `force` an action does not wait: it acts on an element that is visible, enabled and, for typing, editable, and throws for one that is not, where Playwright's would act on it and the browser's answer is not the page's.
- `configure({ testIdAttribute, timeout })`, and `UnsupportedError`.

`expect.element(locator)` takes a locator of the package, with `.not`, `toHaveLength` and `{ timeout }`: it carries Vitest's locator symbol and has the methods `expect.element` calls. A locator is assignable to `Locator` of `vitest/browser`.

Not yet, on the type and throwing with the way out, `vitestPage.elementLocator(await locator.findElement())`: `screenshot`, `mark`, `page.viewport`, `page.screenshot`, `page.mark`, `page.frameLocator`. A locator of `vitest/browser` in `filter({ has })`, `and` or `or` throws too, and so do `upload` of a path, which the page cannot read, and `dropTo` of an element the browser drags itself, `draggable` as a link or an image is: Chrome's drag and drop of HTML.

## Waiting

One look now, one after each batch of changes of the DOM (one `MutationObserver` for the whole wait), one every 50 ms besides, until the deadline: the caller's `timeout`, else the running test's time left (minus Vitest's margin, so the locator is in the error), else `configure({ timeout })`, 1000 ms by default. `findElement()` and every action wait so; `expect.element()` waits through `findElement()`. The test's fake timers do not stop it.

An action waits for one element and that it is visible and, for `click`, `dblClick`, `fill`, `clear` and `selectOptions`, enabled; `fill` and `clear` also editable. A disabled button is not clicked: the action fails after its timeout, naming the locator and what it waited for.

## In a DOM of Node.js

The package runs in happy-dom and in jsdom too, as Vitest's `environment`: the package's suite holds its locators there to ivya, the engine of Vitest's locators, in the same DOM, its actions to Playwright's events in Chromium, step by step, recorded by the browser's fidelity test, and its waits to their tests. What the DOM itself does otherwise is the DOM's: the click of a label on its control, happy-dom's `data` of an `InputEvent`, jsdom's `select` of every caret that moves. Such a DOM lays nothing out and draws nothing, and the package answers so:

- An element is visible by its style alone: no `display: none` on it or above it, and no `visibility: hidden`; a style without a visibility, happy-dom's, is visible.
- A click lands on the element itself, at 0, 0, as there is no box to hit; `wheel` scrolls nothing.
- Pseudo-elements add nothing to a name, as there are no styles of them.
- Events get no `view`, which the window of Vitest's jsdom environment is not to jsdom's events; a DOM without `PointerEvent` or `InputEvent` gets a `MouseEvent` or an `Event`; `isContentEditable` is read from the attribute where jsdom has none; `upload` throws in jsdom, which has no `DataTransfer`.
- The deadline of a wait is the running test's, from Vitest's worker.

The package knows such a DOM by Node.js running it, not by its boxes, which a test of jsdom may mock.

## Speed

The budgets, on a developer's machine, on a page of the notes app with 20 notes: a link by its name, `getByRole("link", { name })`, in 60 µs, with the whole accessible name; a click in 100 µs, with the mouse's way to the element. On a Mac with Chromium: about 64 µs and 86 µs, where Vitest's own take about 166 µs and 50 ms. `test/micro.test.ts` measures them, prints them, and holds the package's to no more than Vitest's in the same run, as a CI runner's speed is its own.
